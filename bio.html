Dancing

I enjoy dancing and have been doing it for years. The main dance that I do is called Bharatanatyam. It is an Indian Classical Dance that originated in Southern India and later diffused to other places. It is an intricate dance with beautiful and precise hand gestures, graceful and sharp body strucutre, ppowerful footwork, and complex footwork It also expresses emotions in an extremly deep manner. As it is one of the first classical dance forms in India, it is heavily influenced by Indian Mythology and beliefs. To me, it helps me connect with my culture. In modern day, this dance form continues to grow while preserving its orignal roots. It is including many other topics liek women empowerment, self-esteem, and other major problems and solutions in society. This dance is a langauge on its own, which people can understand even if they do not know the language. I have been doing this dance for the past 8 years and I have a senior certicate in this course. Other notable mentions of dance that I have done are hiphop, bollywood dancing, and contmeporary.
